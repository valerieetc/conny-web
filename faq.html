Frequently Asked Questions



Q: What is Conny and who is it for?

A: Conny is an AI companion built for neurodivergent individuals. It breaks down tasks into simple steps, adapts routines to your energy and sensory needs, and offers proactive, empathetic support and guidance.

Q: How do I create an account?

A: Download the Conny app on Android, tap Create account, and register with your email or Google sign-in.

Q: How do I use Conny?

A: Just tap the chat icon and say “Hello”, Conny will respond and guide you through setting up routines, checking your calendar, or anything else you need.

Q: Is Conny free to use?

A: Yes, during our beta phase, all features are completely free with no credit card required.

Q: Will I ever need to pay for Conny?

A: We plan to introduce optional premium features in the future. You’ll receive at least 30 days’ notice before any paid plans launch.

Q: How will billing work in the paid version?

A: We’ll support credit/debit cards and Google Pay, and you can easily manage or cancel your subscription in the app’s Settings.

Q: Can I use Conny without internet?

A: Yes, basic routines and lists work offline. For AI chat and syncing data, you’ll need an internet connection.

Q: Is my data safe with Conny?

A: Absolutely! Your data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, and we never sell your information to third parties.

Q: How do I delete my account?

A: Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, confirm your choice, and all your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

Q: What does Conny do that a calendar or a to-do list doesn't?

A: Calendars and lists show you everything at once. Conny shows you one thing. The cognitive load of picking, sequencing, and starting is what executive dysfunction makes hard. Conny absorbs that so you only face the doing.

Q: Can I turn off all reminders?

A: Yes, completely. Conny is consent-based by design. You can use it purely as an on-demand source of the next step, or set a small number of check-ins per day (we cap it at five). There's a "Leave me alone" toggle that works instantly, no explanation required.

Q: Will Conny work if I stop using it for a week?

A: Yes. There's no streak to break, no guilt message when you come back. You pick up exactly where it makes sense to pick up. Conny has no opinion about how often you use it.

Q: Who sees my data?

A: By default, only you. Caregiver access is off unless you turn it on, and you choose what gets shared. We don't sell, aggregate, or profile your data. Full privacy terms are linked in the footer and we're happy to walk you through them.
