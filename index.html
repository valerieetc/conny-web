Most days fall apart in similar places

There's a message you need to reply to. You've already read it. It would take a few minutes to respond. But you've been waiting for days.
You have twenty minutes to leave. You know it. You haven't moved. The phone, the keys, and the shoes are all there. You can see them, but you don't know where to start. So you stay at home.
Someone told you a specific time or name. By the time you need it, it's gone. You forgot it because there was nowhere to write it down and find it at the right moment.

Knowing does not always mean doing

Sometimes the next step is missing. Sometimes you know it, but can’t reach it. ConnyAI is the small thing that helps you get there. It does not plan your day. It does not coach you. It does not judge. It simply tells you the next step and waits.

What ConnyAI does


Splits up your tasks into easy steps

Turns "clean the kitchen" into a sequence you follow without thinking. One step. Then the next.

Helps you to adjust when plans suddenly change

If a meeting moves, Conny adjusts the rest of your day.

Helps you with time blindness

When you can't tell if it's been five minutes or two hours, Conny keeps you oriented.

Provides a calm sensory experience

Low-noise mode, on by default. The page stays calm even when the day is loud.

Keeps your trusted person updated

An optional connection with a person you trust. They see only what you choose to share.

About us

For two years, we have been listening to neurodivergent people describe the moment the brain cannot supply the next step and the day breaks at that line. The tools that exist ask you to plan, prioritize, and decide. But when you cannot do those things, these tools stop working. ConnyAI fills that space. Every rule the product follows is there because someone described a specific moment where it would have helped.
